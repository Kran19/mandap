# Open Business Questions for Client Confirmation

1. **Stock Catalog Sizes**: Is stock available in every integer size from 1–20 ft, or only specific standard catalog sizes (e.g., 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 20 ft)?
2. **Intermediate Pole Spacing Strategy**: For a 35 ft run, should the intermediate support be placed exactly at a truss joint, at 30 ft, or centered at 17.5 ft?
3. **Corner Pole Rules**: Do open-ended runs or internal T-junctions require vertical poles unconditionally?
4. **Vertical Pole Stock**: Do vertical support poles pull from the exact same truss piece inventory as horizontal beams?
5. **Inventory-Aware Optimizer**: In Phase 1, design optimization determines required BOM independent of stock levels, and inventory validator flags shortages. Should a future mode alter the piece decomposition if preferred stock is missing?
